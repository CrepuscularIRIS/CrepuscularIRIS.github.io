# kh: 3D Shape Symmetry via Chirality Disentanglement

**Authors:** Weikang Wang, Tobias Weißberg, Nafie El Amrani, Florian Bernard | **ICCV 2025**

---

## One-Line Summary

This paper learns to **split 3D shape representations into chirality-symmetric and chirality-asymmetric components**, giving neural networks an explicit handle on mirror symmetry and handedness — a capability that current shape encoders largely ignore.

## Why This Is Worth Your Time

Most 3D vision models treat a left hand and a right hand as "similar but not identical" through metric learning. This paper asks: **what if we explicitly model why they differ?** By disentangling chirality — the geometric property that distinguishes an object from its mirror image — the framework moves beyond similarity scores to **structured geometric reasoning**. For anyone building world models or embodied agents, this is a direct upgrade to how machines represent physical objects.

## Core Method (3-Minute Version)

The core intuition is borrowed from physics and chemistry: **chirality is not a deformation, it is a fundamental invariant**. A molecule's handedness determines its biological activity; a 3D object's handedness determines its functional affordances. The authors design a representation learning framework that factorizes shape embeddings into two orthogonal subspaces:

- **A chirality-symmetric component** capturing properties invariant under reflection (size, topology, coarse geometry).
- **A chirality-asymmetric component** capturing handedness-specific structure (twist, orientation of asymmetric features).

This disentanglement is enforced through a combination of **equivariance constraints** and **contrastive objectives**: the symmetric subspace must remain identical for an object and its mirror image, while the asymmetric subspace must flip sign or structure in a predictable way. The result is a representation where **mirror symmetry becomes an explicit algebraic operation rather than an implicit statistical tendency**.

The framework is architecture-agnostic — it wraps around standard point cloud or mesh encoders and only modifies the representation head and loss landscape. This means it can be dropped into existing pipelines for shape matching, retrieval, or generation without retraining the backbone.

## Further Reading

- **Symmetry-aware shape analysis** has a long lineage in geometry processing; this work bridges it with modern self-supervised representation learning. For context, see the classic work on **intrinsic symmetry detection** (e.g., Ovsjanikov et al., 2008) and recent **equivariant neural networks for 3D data** (e.g., SE(3)-Transformers, Vector Neurons).
